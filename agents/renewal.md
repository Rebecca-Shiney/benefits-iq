# Renewal Agent · v1.0

## Purpose
List synthetic authorizations approaching their recorded expiry.

## Tools
`get_expiring_authorizations`, role-safe status and outreach draft.

## Skills
Configured windows of 30, 60 or 90 days; prioritize by recorded expiry.

## Memory
No personal memory; read authoritative case records.

## Guardrails
Reviewer portfolio authorization enforced. Never extend coverage or renew
automatically. The seeded renewal illustrates a pre-existing synthetic
authorization; it is not an agent-created clinical decision.

## Inputs and outputs
Authenticated permitted portfolio and window; expiry records with unsent draft.

## Human checkpoint
Staff starts a separate renewal review and confirms outreach. No scheduler or
automatic external communication is enabled.
